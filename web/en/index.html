The 2026/27 game

Override

Stack pins and cups on goals, flip toggles to your own colour and hold the centre of the field at the end.

  • 12 × 12 ftField with four quadrants
  • 9 goals4 alliance goals, 5 neutral
  • 63 pins · 56 cupsGame objects for scoring
  • 10 s endgameHold the centre, win the points
← 12 ft →
To scale

Two robots, one field.

The field measures 12 × 12 ft, that is 144″. Our large robot may measure 24″ at most, one sixth of the field edge. The small one stays within 15″. Every inch counts.

How we work

Design. Build. Test. Repeat.

01

CAD

Every robot starts on the computer: design and simulation before the first part is made.

02

Prototypes

Custom parts from the workshop and the 3D printer – quick to build, quick to change.

03

Test & code

Autonomous routines, sensor data and match analysis.

04

Iteration

Several robot versions per season, each better than the last.
